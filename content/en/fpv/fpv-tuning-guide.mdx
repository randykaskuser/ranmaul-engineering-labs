# FPV Tuning for Cinematic Footage

Getting smooth FPV drone footage requires precise PID (Proportional, Integral, Derivative) tuning. This article documents field constraints and tuning solutions for a 5-inch drone frame.

## Field Challenges

Variable wind and prop-wash are the two main enemies in cinematic flight. When a drone descends through its own exhaust air (prop-wash), the image can shake violently.

<Warning>
Do not increase the D (Derivative) value too high. Although it can reduce prop-wash, an excessively high D value can cause the motors to become very hot and potentially burn out!
</Warning>

## Baseline Parameters

Here are the baseline parameters we use as a starting point:

- **Proportional (P):** Slightly lower than freestyle tuning for slower, more controlled movements.
- **Integral (I):** High enough to maintain the angle and resist strong winds during stable flight (cruising).
- **Derivative (D):** Kept within safe limits to dampen prop-wash without excessively raising motor temperatures.
