# SOP for Treating Cloudy Eye in Predator Fish

Cloudy eye strikes dense predator tanks fast. It destroys vision. It indicates toxic water. 

Many hobbyists dump salt in the tank and wait. This approach fails. The fish continue to suffer. You need a precise chemical and physical intervention.

## Why Salt Fails

Fish excrete 80 to 90 percent of their ammonia through their gills. Fasting the fish does not stop this process. Concentrated ammonia builds up. It constantly burns the fish's eyes. Salt does not neutralize ammonia. 

Medication behavior differs from salt behavior. Salt stays in the water. You only replace salt lost during water changes. Medications break down within 24 hours. You must dose medications for the entire tank volume every day. 

## The Intervention Strategy

You must stop the ammonia burn. You must protect the wound. You must manage osmotic stress. 

We use three specific tools. 

1. **Aquarium Salt**: Reduces osmotic stress.
2. **Seachem AmGuard**: Binds toxic ammonia instantly. It leaves biological filters intact. 
3. **Seachem StressGuard**: Binds to proteins in eye wounds. It acts as a liquid bandage.

## Dosing Assumptions and Formulas

Calculate your exact tank volume before starting. Use these baseline formulas for all dosages. 

* **Aquarium Salt**: 2 grams per liter of total tank water.
* **Seachem AmGuard**: 0.025 ml per liter of total tank water.
* **Seachem StressGuard**: 0.13 ml per liter of total tank water.

## Treatment Protocols

Choose the protocol matching your tank volume. Follow these steps exactly. 

### Protocol A: 100 Liter Tank

**Day 1: Crisis Reset**
* Drain 60L of water. Siphon all bottom waste.
* Dissolve 200 grams of salt in a separate container. Pour it into the tank gradually. 
* Pour 2.5 ml of AmGuard into the filter flow. 
* Pour 13 ml of StressGuard into the tank. 
* Turn off all lights. Fast the fish. 

**Day 2 to Day 6: Daily Maintenance**
* Drain 30L of water. Siphon bottom waste. Do this at the same time every day.
* Add 60 grams of new salt. This replaces salt lost in the removed water.
* Pour 2.5 ml of AmGuard. This treats the full 100L volume.
* Pour 13 ml of StressGuard. This treats the full 100L volume.
* Keep lights off. Keep fasting the fish. 

### Protocol B: 300 Liter Tank

**Day 1: Crisis Reset**
* Drain 180L of water. Siphon all bottom waste.
* Dissolve 600 grams of salt. Pour it in multiple sessions.
* Pour 7.5 ml of AmGuard into the tank. 
* Pour 39 ml of StressGuard into the tank. 
* Turn off all lights. Fast the fish. 

**Day 2 to Day 6: Daily Maintenance**
* Drain 90L of water. Siphon bottom waste.
* Add 180 grams of new salt. 
* Pour 7.5 ml of AmGuard. This treats the full 300L volume.
* Pour 39 ml of StressGuard. This treats the full 300L volume.
* Keep lights off. Keep fasting the fish.

## Critical Mistakes to Avoid

Do not use additional water stabilizers. AmGuard removes chlorine and chloramine. Extra chemicals create toxic concentrations.

Do not pour bacteria starter during treatment. Daily water changes waste the expensive bacteria. Save the starter for the recovery phase.

Do not perform 50 percent water changes every day. Massive daily changes cause severe pH and temperature swings. This crashes the fish's immune system. Stick to the 30 percent daily rhythm.

## Recovery Phase 

Start this phase when the eye membrane turns clear. 

1. Perform a 50 percent water change. This removes medication residue and excess salt.
2. Stop dosing AmGuard and StressGuard.
3. Pour bacteria starter into the tank. Use the standard dose. This rebuilds the biological filter.
4. Feed the fish. Start with very small portions.
