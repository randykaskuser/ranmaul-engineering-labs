# Channa Andrao: Mini Predator with Vibrant Blue Colors

## Summary

Channa andrao is one of the most popular dwarf snakeheads among predator fish hobbyists due to its small size, striking metallic blue color, and interesting territorial aggressive character.


---

## Natural Habitat

Channa andrao originates from northeastern India, specifically the areas of:
- Assam
- West Bengal
- Shallow waters with slow currents

Its natural habitat features:
- Abundant aquatic plants
- Dried leaves
- Tendency towards acidic pH
- Relatively cool temperatures compared to large tropical snakeheads


---

## Size and Character

| Parameter | Description |
|---|---|
| Adult Size | ±10–12 cm |
| Temperament | Semi-aggressive |
| Care Level | Intermediate |
| Suitable for | Species tank |

Despite its small size, Channa andrao remains a territorial predator.

Do not be fooled by its small size:
- They can still kill each other
- Especially when adult
- Or when space is too confined


---

## Recommended Aquarium Setup

### Minimum Tank


```plain text
60 x 30 x 30 cm
```

For a pair:


```plain text
80 cm tank is safer
```

### Aquascape

It is recommended to use:
- Driftwood
- Floating plants
- Rocks
- PVC hiding spots
- Ketapang (Indian Almond) leaves

The goals are:
- Reducing stress
- Breaking line of sight
- Lowering aggression


---

## Water Parameters

Dwarf snakeheads prefer:
- Still water
- Dim lighting
- Dark/natural environment


---

## Diet

Favorite foods:
- Worms
- Small crickets
- Shrimp
- Frozen bloodworms
- High protein pellets

Avoid:
- Overfeeding
- Cheap feeder fish
- Continuous high-fat food

Because it can cause:
- Fatty liver
- Obesity
- Rapid deterioration of water quality


---

## Interesting Behavior

One of the most interesting things about Channa andrao is:

> Color changes depending on mood and dominance.

When conditions are optimal:
- Metallic blue emerges
- Fins become brighter
- Body patterns become more contrasted

When stressed:
- Pale color
- More passive
- Frequent hiding


---

## Tankmates?

Not recommended for community tanks.

Best choice:


```plain text
species only tank
```

If forced into a community:
- Risk of damaged fins
- Stress
- Territorial killing

The risks are quite high.


---

## Conclusion

Channa andrao is suitable for predator hobbyists who:
- Enjoy behavior observation
- Like natural blackwater setups
- Want a small snakehead with unique colors

Keys to successful maintenance:
- Stable water quality
- Plenty of hiding spots
- Do not overstock
- Control aggression from a young age
