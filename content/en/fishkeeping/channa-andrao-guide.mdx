# Channa Andrao: Mini Predator with Vibrant Blue Colors

## Summary

Channa andrao is one of the most popular dwarf snakeheads among predator fish hobbyists due to its small size, striking metallic blue coloration, and fascinating aggressive territorial character.

---

## Natural Habitat

Channa andrao originates from northeastern India, primarily in the following areas:
- Assam
- West Bengal
- Shallow waters with slow currents

Its natural habitat features:
- Abundant aquatic plants
- Leaf litter
- Acidic pH levels
- Relatively cool temperatures compared to larger tropical snakeheads

---

## Size and Character

| Parameter | Description |
|---|---|
| Adult Size | ±10–12 cm |
| Temperament | Semi-aggressive |
| Care Level | Intermediate |
| Best Suited For | Species tank |

Despite its small size, Channa andrao remains a territorial predator.

Do not be fooled by its small stature:
- They can still kill each other
- Especially when reaching adulthood
- Or when the space is too confined

---

## Recommended Aquarium Setup

### Minimum Tank

```text
60 x 30 x 30 cm
```

For a pair:

```plain text
80 cm tank is safer
```

### Aquascape

It is recommended to use:
- Driftwood
- Floating plants
- Rocks
- PVC hiding spots
- Indian Almond leaves (Catappa)

The goals are:
- Reducing stress
- Breaking the line of sight
- Lowering aggression

---

## Water Parameters

Dwarf snakeheads prefer:
- Still water
- Dim lighting
- Dark/natural environments

---

## Diet and Feeding

Favorite foods:
- Earthworms
- Small crickets
- Shrimp
- Frozen bloodworms
- High-protein pellets

Avoid:
- Overfeeding
- Low-quality feeder fish
- Continuous high-fat foods

Because these can cause:
- Fatty liver
- Obesity
- Rapid deterioration of water quality

---

## Interesting Behavior

One of the most fascinating aspects of Channa andrao is:

> Color changes depending on mood and dominance.

When conditions are optimal:
- Metallic blue emerges
- Fins become brighter
- Body patterns show higher contrast

When stressed:
- Colors become pale
- More passive
- Frequent hiding

---

## Tankmates?

Not recommended for community tanks.

The best choice is:

```plain text
species only tank
```

If forced into a community setup:
- Risk of damaged fins
- Stress
- Territorial killings

The risks are quite high.

---

## Conclusion

Channa andrao is suitable for predator hobbyists who:
- Enjoy behavioral observation
- Like natural blackwater setups
- Want a small snakehead with unique colors

Keys to successful maintenance:
- Stable water quality
- Plenty of hiding spots
- Do not overstock
- Control aggression from a young age
