# Water Parameter Management

Maintaining an aquarium ecosystem (fishkeeping) is essentially the art of maintaining water quality. The fish are merely indicators of how well the water system is managed.

## Critical Parameters

Monitoring the following parameters is essential for long-term stability:

1. **Ammonia (NH3) & Nitrite (NO2):** Must always be zero (0 ppm).
2. **Nitrate (NO3):** Kept below 20-40 ppm through routine water changes or aquatic plant filtration (aquaponics).
3. **pH & Hardness (GH/KH):** Adjusted to the species being kept. Stability is prioritized over fluctuating ideal numbers.

## Maintenance Loop

We implement a measurement-based weekly maintenance cycle, where water changes are performed based on the logged Nitrate increase.

<Note>
Biological filtration takes time to stabilize. Avoid cleaning filter media with tap water (chlorine) as it can kill beneficial bacterial colonies.
</Note>
